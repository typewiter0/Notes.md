### Core Concepts & Natural Resources

> **Human-Environmental Interaction**  
> The dual connection and exchange between humans and the natural world, covering how humans alter physical environments and how environments impact human societies[cite: 6].

> **Natural Resources:** Items occurring in the natural environment that people find useful or beneficial (e.g., air, water, oil, fish, soil, minerals)[cite: 6].

### Sustainability & Land Use

-   **Sustainability:** Using current resources in ways that preserve them for future generations while minimizing negative environmental impacts.
-   **Sustainable Policies:** Encouraging a transition toward renewable, lower-emission energy while reducing dependence on non-renewable fossil fuels.
-   **Land Use:** Studying how people utilize, modify, and organize land.
    -   **Natural Environment:** Untouched natural elements such as plants, air, water, and wildlife.
    -   **Built Environment:** Physical human-made artifacts on the landscape (e.g., buildings, roads, signs, farms, fences).
    -   **Cultural Landscape:** The unique built environment created when human cultures interact with their distinct physical surroundings.
